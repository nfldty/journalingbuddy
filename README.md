Journaling Buddy is your thoughtful AI companion that helps you reflect on your day—not just by reading your entries, but by talking to you about them. Write down your thoughts in your personal journal, and our empathetic AI avatar will gently ask how you're feeling and encourage deeper reflection, just like a mindful friend.

✨ Features
📝 Personal Journaling – Write and save your private journal entries on your own secure space.

🧍‍♀️ Interactive AI Avatar – After you write, the AI reads your journal and starts a reflective conversation with you.

🗣️ Voice Interaction – Speak with the avatar and express your thoughts out loud—because sometimes it feels better to say it than write it.

🌱 Emotional Insight – The AI asks thoughtful questions to help you explore your emotions, behaviors, and patterns.

🔐 Privacy First – Journals are personal; everything stays between you and your buddy.

💡 Why Journaling Buddy?
We believe that journaling is powerful—but reflecting with someone can be even more meaningful. Journaling Buddy offers a supportive, human-like experience where your AI gently nudges you to explore your day, emotions, and personal growth.
